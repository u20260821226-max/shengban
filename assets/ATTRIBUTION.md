# Bundled assets

- Piano samples: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm, CC BY 3.0. Source: https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/ (repository: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander). These local MP3 samples are pitch-shifted for MIDI playback; no vocal synthesis is used.
- Music engraving: OpenSheetMusicDisplay, BSD-3-Clause. License in `vendor/OSMD-LICENSE.txt`.
- Score and backup audio files: supplied by the project owner. Playback follows the supplied MIDI, including any existing recognition/editing errors; the app does not certify musical accuracy.


## 2026-10-27 页面视觉资源

`assets/visuals/mountain-lake.webp` 为本项目通过图像生成工具制作的雾山湖景。三个 `*-cover.jpg` 为项目发起人提供并指定用于三首预置曲目展示的图片；图片保留原始内容和标记，页面仅通过 CSS 调整裁切、亮度、饱和度与遮罩。图片不表示网站获得歌手、电影或版权方的背书。
